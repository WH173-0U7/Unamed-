# DarcVoyd: lore

Notes on the world, for building the game. Nothing here is shown to the
player unless the game itself chooses to reveal it.

## The Whisp

The small orb of light the player controls.

- It exists only through the player's touch: it appears wherever they touch
  the darkness and dies away when they let go.
- It feeds on that touch. Every movement of the player's thumb gives it life,
  making it glow brighter and reach further into the dark. Left alone it
  fades back to an ember.
- As it feeds, threads of light are drawn out of the player's fingertip and
  into the Whisp.
- Its name is never displayed in the game.

## The darkness

Where the game begins: a very dark place of rock, cracks and drifting fog,
visible only where the Whisp's light reaches.

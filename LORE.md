# DarcVoyd: lore

Notes on the world, for building the game. Nothing here is shown to the
player unless the game itself chooses to reveal it.

## Current direction

- The game begins with a television in the dark. Turning it on reveals a map.
- The map is an endless, procedurally generated city grid. The character
  moves across it one square at a time.

## Scrapped ideas (kept for reference)

These were built and then set aside. The code is still in the project's
history if they are ever wanted again.

- **The Whisp:** a small orb of light that appeared under the player's touch,
  fed on their movement to glow brighter, and travelled through the world.
- **The cave:** an endless network of tunnels and chambers like a nervous
  system, dark, cold and misty, with walls that breathed.
- **The organism:** a vast living background behind the cave, with currents,
  pulsing veins and slow breathing, as if inside something much larger.

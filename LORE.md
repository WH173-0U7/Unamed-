# DarcVoyd: lore

Notes on the world, for building the game. Nothing here is shown to the
player unless the game itself chooses to reveal it.

## The Whisp

The small orb of light the player controls.

- It exists only through the player's touch: it appears wherever they touch
  the darkness and dies away when they let go.
- It feeds on that touch. Every movement of the player's thumb gives it life,
  making it glow brighter and reach further into the dark. Left alone it
  fades back to an ember.
- As it feeds, threads of light are drawn out of the player's fingertip and
  into the Whisp.
- It travels: it is pulled through the cave towards wherever the player's
  touch is, and the world slides past around it. It cannot pass through rock.
- Its name is never displayed in the game.

## The cave

The world the Whisp travels through: an endless network of chambers and
tunnels, like the inside of a nervous system. Dark, cold and misty.

- Chambers are like nerve cells; tunnels join them like the connections
  between neurons, and thin dead-end branches reach off each chamber.
- Every chamber is connected to the rest: nowhere is truly cut off.
- The walls are wet, fibrous and cold. They breathe, slowly widening and
  narrowing in waves that travel along the tunnels.
- Cold mist hangs in the passages.
- Nothing is visible except where the Whisp's light reaches; the rock blocks
  its light and casts shadows.
- The whole cave is generated, not designed: it goes on forever and is the
  same every time it is visited.

## The depths: the organism

Behind and beneath the cave is a vast emptiness that moves on its own. It
feels like being inside something far larger, like being part of a living
organism.

- Slow currents flow through it; vast shadows drift past.
- A web of veins far below glows faintly with a slow heartbeat.
- The whole of it breathes in and out.
- It is seen through the open tunnels, and faintly through the walls.
